Purchasing & Replenishment Management System

Global Supplies PRMS

One workspace for Global Supplies staff to review vendor inventory, match products to the catalogue and track changes in availability.

Vendor inventory

Import vendor spreadsheets using saved profiles for each file layout. Review validation results and keep the original files for traceability.

Catalogue matching

Match UPCs, catalogue item numbers and approved SKU mappings. Uncertain matches go to a review queue for an authorized person to resolve.

Stock availability

Review stock by vendor, watch unavailable products and see which vendor supplied a new availability update. Connect read-only Amazon and Nineyard data.

Read-only Gmail inventory imports

A mailbox owner can authorize PRMS to read the receiving Gmail account so approved vendor attachments and download links can become inventory imports. An administrator chooses approved senders, file rules and import profiles.

The Google permission is read-only access to the authorized mailbox. PRMS uses message metadata to route approved vendor messages and may read their bodies and attachments to retrieve inventory files. Sender rules and labels filter processing; they do not narrow the mailbox permission granted by Google.

PRMS does not send email or edit or delete Gmail messages. The owner can revoke the connection in their Google Account. The privacy policy explains data use, storage, retention and deletion requests.

Access for authorized Global Supplies staff

Operational screens require an approved PRMS account. This public information page contains no mailbox messages, vendor files or inventory records.

For access, support or questions about this integration, contact your Global Supplies administrator or the user support email displayed on the Google authorization screen.
